Which voice sounds better?

Compare anonymous voices reading the same sentence. Keep your playback volume consistent. Rate each voice on its own; the letters are reshuffled for every sentence.

Clarity: 1 = hard to understand, 5 = easy to understand. Naturalness: 1 = very artificial, 5 = natural. Pronunciation: 1 = many errors, 5 = correct sounds, stress and tones. These are exploratory listening ratings, not a formal MOS study.
